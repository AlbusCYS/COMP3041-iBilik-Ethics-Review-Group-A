# 4. Privacy, Profiling and Personal-Data Use

## 4.1 Further Investigation and Evidence
Part 1 findings (recap). Advert cookies loaded with no banner, sign-up had no consent box, and page code hid landlords' phone numbers (Group A, 2026; E6, E7).

New in Part 2:

Outdated: The policy still says "last updated on 3 May 2021", despite the 2026 site rebuild (iBilik, n.d.; S01).

Contradictory: It claims to honour Do Not Track (a browser "don't track me" setting), then says "we allow third-party behavioural tracking" (S02). It promises anonymous visits, yet Google shows adverts "based on previous visits" (S03, S04). That is profiling: tracking behaviour to guess interests.

Incomplete: It never mentions the IC or passport copies taken for the "KYC Verified" (ID-checked) badge (E8).

Wrong app label: Google Play says iBilikPlus 2.0 has "No data collected", but Apple lists contact info and identifiers (iBilik Dot Com Sdn Bhd, 2026; GFG Tech, 2026; S05, S06).

Behind the law: Malaysia's amended data law (PDPA) now requires serious breaches to be reported within 72 hours, and some firms need a data protection officer (DPO) (Personal Data Protection Commissioner [PDPC], 2025a; S08). iBilik still says "within 30 business days" and names no DPO (S07). Profiling for advertising is one trigger for needing a DPO (PDPC, 2025b; S09).
