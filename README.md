## 1. Project Overview

This project builds upon our Part 1 investigation of iBilik, a Malaysian accommodation and rental platform, alongside iBilikPlus.

Part 1 identified four main ethical issues:

1. Discrimination and Unequal Access to Housing
2. Safety, Trust and Platform Responsibility
3. Privacy, Profiling and Personal-Data Use
4. Financial Fairness, Transparency and Accountability

Part 2 expands upon these findings through further investigation, stakeholder analysis, application of the Software Engineering Code of Ethics (SE Code), ethical theories, critical reflection and practical recommendations.

The objective is to provide an evidence-based ethical assessment of iBilik and propose realistic improvements.

## 3. Requirements for Each Mini-Report

Each member should structure their mini-report using the following subsections.

### 3.1 Further Investigation

Build upon the evidence collected in Part 1.

Include:
- Additional firsthand investigation
- Relevant screenshots and platform observations
- Additional supporting academic literature
- Comparison between Part 1 and Part 2 findings

Clearly distinguish newly collected evidence from previous findings.

### 3.2 Stakeholder Analysis

Identify the stakeholders involved, including:

- Direct users
- iBilik and its developers
- Property owners or advertisers
- Business partners
- Indirectly affected individuals

Explain:
- Who is affected?
- How are they affected?
- Which platform features cause these effects?
- What evidence supports the analysis?

### 3.3 SE Code of Ethics Analysis

Apply relevant clauses from the ACM/IEEE-CS Software Engineering Code of Ethics.

For each clause:

1. Identify the specific clause.
2. Explain why it applies.
3. Evaluate whether iBilik aligns with, partially meets or diverges from it.
4. Support the judgement with evidence.
5. Consider reasonable counterarguments.

Use the structure:

**Claim → Evidence → Reasoning → Counterpoint**

Avoid simply describing or quoting the Code.

### 3.4 Ethical Reflection

Discuss:

- Ethical dilemmas raised by the issue
- Competing stakeholder interests
- Potential conflicts between professional responsibilities
- Limitations of the SE Code
- Possible unintended consequences of proposed solutions

Focus on critical reasoning rather than repeating earlier findings.

### 3.5 Recommendations

Suggest realistic improvements to iBilik.

Each recommendation should explain:

- What should change?
- Why is the change necessary?
- Which ethical problem would it address?
- How would stakeholders benefit?
- What potential trade-offs could arise?

Recommendations should be supported by evidence and ethical reasoning.

### 3.6 References and Evidence

- Use APA 7 for citations and references.
- Prioritise relevant academic literature.
- Cite platform policies and firsthand evidence where appropriate.
- Upload relevant screenshots and supporting materials to GitHub.
- Ensure every important factual claim is supported.

## 5. Ethical Framework

### Primary Framework

ACM/IEEE-CS Software Engineering Code of Ethics and Professional Practice.

Relevant clauses identified in Part 1:

| Issue | SE Code Clauses |
|---|---|
| Discrimination | 1.02, 1.07, 3.03 |
| Safety and Trust | 1.03, 3.10 |
| Privacy and Personal Data | 3.12, 3.13 |
| Financial Fairness | 1.06, 6.07 |

Members should verify the wording and applicability of each clause and may include additional relevant clauses.

### Supporting Ethical Theories

**Utilitarianism**
- Evaluates consequences and overall well-being.
- Considers benefits, harms and stakeholder trade-offs.

**Deontology**
- Evaluates moral duties, rights and principles.
- Emphasises fairness, autonomy and respect for individuals.

The group leader will apply both theories to the same ethical dilemma, compare their conclusions and explain why the SE Code alone is insufficient.

## 6. Group Leader Sections

### 6.1 Introduction

- Introduce iBilik and iBilikPlus.
- Explain their purpose and target users.
- Summarise the findings from Part 1.
- Explain how Part 2 extends the investigation.
- Introduce the ethical frameworks applied.

### 6.2 Ethical Theory Comparison

Primary dilemma:

**Demographic preferences versus equal access to housing.**

Discuss:

1. Why the SE Code cannot fully resolve the dilemma.
2. How utilitarianism evaluates potential benefits and harms.
3. How deontology evaluates fairness, rights and duties.
4. Where the theories agree or disagree.
5. Which approach provides the stronger justification.
6. The group's final ethical judgement.

Additional dilemma to consider:

**Identity verification versus personal-data privacy.**

### 6.3 Overall Critical Reflection

Reflect on:

- How our understanding developed from Part 1.
- The responsibilities of developers versus platform operators.
- Conflicts between ethical principles.
- The limitations of professional codes.
- How ethical theories influence our decisions.
- Limitations of our investigation and evidence.

### 6.4 Integrated Recommendations and Conclusion

Combine the recommendations from all four issues.

Ensure that recommendations are:

- Practical
- Evidence-based
- Ethically justified
- Consistent across issues
- Sensitive to competing stakeholder interests

Conclude with the group's overall ethical assessment of iBilik.
